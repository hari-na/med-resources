# General Surgery Clinical Cases. Resources

## Knowledge

- **S Das. A Manual on Clinical Surgery, 13th edition (Feb 2018)**, `pdfs/` (local)
  The primary source for history and examination. Chapters are organised by region and by
  presenting problem, and the text already attaches reasoning to each item (age, occupation,
  religion, residence). Use for: every history and examination section, and the special tests.
  Split to plain text per chapter under `surgery/sources/sdas/`, each page marked
  `[[ book p.N | pdf p.N ]]` so citations are exact.

- **Rajamahendran. Long Cases / Short Cases (Surgery)**, `pdfs/Rajamahendran_Long.pdf`, `pdfs/Rajamahendran_Short.pdf`
  Already indexed in this repo's search. Use for: how an Indian examiner frames the case,
  and for case-presentation wording.

- **Bailey & Love. Short Practice of Surgery, 28th edition**, *not yet available*
  Use for: management, investigations, staging, operative principles.

- **SRB. Manual of Surgery, 8th edition**, *not yet available*
  Use for: Indian exam-oriented management detail and viva answers.

## Gaps

- Bailey & Love and SRB are the management sources, and both are still missing. Until they
  land, management sections are written from established surgical practice and carry no page
  citation; each is marked so it can be verified and cited once the books are indexed.
- S Das here is the 13th edition, not the 17th Hitha named. Citations therefore give chapter
  and section heading as well as page, so they survive the edition difference.

## Wisdom

- **The White Army (YouTube)**, https://youtube.com/@thewhitearmy
  Recommended by a senior for case demonstrations. Use for: seeing a case presented aloud
  before presenting it yourself.
