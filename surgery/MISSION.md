# Mission: General Surgery clinical cases (MBBS final year)

## Why
Hitha sits her MBBS final-year surgery examinations, clinics, viva and theory. The
examiner's question is almost never "what is the diagnosis"; it is "why did you ask
that?" and "what would that finding rule out?". She needs every history question and
every physical sign to come with its reasoning attached, so that under questioning she
can defend the step rather than recite the list.

## Success looks like
- Given any of the long cases, she can take a history where every question has a stated purpose
- For each physical sign, she can say what it indicates and what it excludes
- She can order investigations and justify each one, rather than listing a panel
- She can state management with the rationale for each decision
- She can survive rapid-fire viva on any case in the set

## Constraints
- Reads on an iPad; documents must print cleanly to PDF
- Examiners expect S Das / Bailey & Love / SRB framing, not international guidelines
- Swarna is studying the same way for medicine, OBG, paediatrics, ENT and ophthalmology,
  so the format should transfer to her subjects

## Out of scope
- Operative technique beyond what a final-year student is asked to describe
- Research-level evidence review; the textbooks named above are the authority
